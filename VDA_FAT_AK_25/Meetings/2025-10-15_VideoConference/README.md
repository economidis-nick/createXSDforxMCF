## 5 &mdash; Open Requirements exemplary overview, Opportunities & Prioritization

CONTACT Software introduces to the &chi;MCF issue list, 
which is maintained at 
[GitHub](../../../../issues).
<!-- https://github.com/economidis-nick/createXSDforxMCF/issues -->

Currently, 73 issues are closed and 28 issues are open.  
The open issues cover a wide range of topics and vary greatly in terms of difficulty.  

As an example of an issue, T-Systems International goes into detail about 
[#106](https://github.com/economidis-nick/createXSDforxMCF/issues/106). 
It addresses so-called "nut plates" (french: "Ecrou à river"), frequently used in aerospace industries.  
CONTACT Software sketches an idea how to describe such connections in &chi;MCF 
in a flexible and sustainable way. 
This idea can be seen in relation to issue 
[#61](https://github.com/economidis-nick/createXSDforxMCF/issues/61)
which requests the support of catalogs of standard joining elements.

Boeing Co. supports this topic and emphasizes 
that the reuse of connector definitions is very relevant for aerospace industries, in general. 
For example, a Boeing&nbsp;747 contains approximately 3 million connections.

[ ] The members of the working group should identify those issues that are most relevant for practical application 
so that work on them can begin as soon as possible.
